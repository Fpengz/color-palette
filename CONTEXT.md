# Chromix domain glossary

## Target

The color the user wants to make. A target may be entered manually or selected
from a full-frame palette or a region of an uploaded image.

## Target capture

The act of turning an uploaded image or manual color entry into a target,
including the source and any region information needed to explain that choice.

## Material

An available color-bearing input with inventory, cost, and tint-strength
properties. Materials are the inputs from which a recipe is made.

## Recipe

A mass-conserving set of material doses for a requested batch. A recipe is
dispensable only when it respects inventory, scale, and shop-floor rules.

## Formulation

The process of finding and explaining a recipe for a target from the available
materials. Formulation includes color fit, operational constraints, and target
reachability.

## Calibration

Measured evidence that connects material behavior to a physical formulation.
The current digital RGB formulation is an estimate until calibration evidence
and held-out validation are available.

## Reachability

The explanation of why a target is matched, approximated, or out of reach for
the current material set, inventory, constraints, or dispensing scale.
